In August, Luanti had a stand at [FrOSCon](https://froscon.org), a
free and open-source conference held in Sankt Augustin, Germany.

<!-- more -->

## The stand

Our stand featured a desktop computer allowing visitors to try out various games and servers.
Alongside keyboard and mouse, visitors were able to try out our new gamepad support.
The stand was later supplemented with a second laptop to increase capacity. We put up two posters behind our
stand, one of which was designed from scratch by ZenonSeth.

Our stand also had plenty of Luanti stickers, which were popular as ever; by the end, most of them had gone.

{% include figure_gallery.html items=page.stand %}


## People we talked to

Our stand was visited by various people, ranging from casual visitors to
regular players, parents, and Luanti community members.

While many were drawn in by apparent similarities to Minecraft, they were
subsequently surprised to discover a variety of games and mods that demonstrate
Luanti's customizability and flexibility. At the same time, certain limitations
of the engine became apparent, highlighting the lack of important features such
as SSCSMs.

We were also approached by parents who are interested in using Luanti at home.
Many of them would like to see more resources for Luanti, particularly video
tutorials for modding.


## Beyond x86

[Nura (formerly postmarketOS)](https://nura.eco/), a Linux distribution targeting
mobile devices, also had a stand at FrOSCon. We were glad to see that Luanti
was used for demonstration. Similar to FOSDEM, we noticed interest in making
Luanti available on Linux mobile, including distributions that deviate further
from "traditional" ones targeting PCs.

[T2/Linux](https://t2linux.com/), which had a stand in the same corridor as
ours, showed us a RISC-V laptop running Luanti. They proceeded to surprise us
as they were able to successfully build and run Luanti on a PowerPC Mac.

## Children's Workshop by Teckids

[Teckids](https://teckids.org/en) is a non-profit that we collaborated with at
FOSDEM. During FrOSCon, they independently held a Luanti-related
[workshop](https://froscon.org/programm/kinder/) for children, teaching them
how to create their own mods.


## Conclusion

This was the first time we attended FrOSCon. We appreciate the positive
experience there, and we would love to attend FrOSCon again next year.

We would like to thank our community for supporting us, particularly with the
content that we were able to present to our visitors.
